Lead Story

Nobel Prize Winner John Jumper Leaves Google DeepMind for Anthropic

The AI talent war just claimed its highest-profile defection yet: the scientist whose AlphaFold work won the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry is walking out of DeepMind's door and into Anthropic's.

John Jumper, a vice president at Google DeepMind and one of the most decorated scientists in modern AI, announced he is leaving to join Anthropic PBC. Jumper shared the Nobel Prize in Chemistry in 2024 alongside DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis for AlphaFold's revolutionary protein structure predictions — work that fundamentally changed computational biology. At DeepMind, he had more recently been central to the company's AI coding and software-engineering efforts.

The departure is a significant blow to Google. Jumper's move comes as Anthropic has quietly become the talent magnet of the industry, fueled by the runaway success of Claude Code and a near-trillion-dollar valuation. According to Bloomberg and CNBC, DeepMind leadership was informed of his decision last week — the same week Anthropic confidentially filed for an IPO.

The timing is pointed. Just days earlier, Hassabis and Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei stood side by side at the G7 summit calling for a united U.S.-led AI coalition. The display of inter-lab diplomacy at the policy level now sits in sharp contrast to the fiercely competitive talent dynamics playing out behind the scenes.

Models

OpenAI's GPT-5.5 Instant Becomes the New Default — Hallucinations Drop 52.5%

OpenAI has made GPT-5.5 Instant its primary default model across ChatGPT and the API. The company says the model achieves a 52.5% reduction in hallucinations across high-stakes domains — medicine, law, and finance — compared to its predecessor. GPT-5.5 Pro and GPT-5.5 variants are also available for tasks requiring deeper reasoning. The hallucination reduction figure is the most significant reliability claim OpenAI has published since GPT-4's launch.

Business

Anthropic Files Confidentially for IPO at Near-Trillion-Dollar Valuation

Anthropic has confidentially filed for an initial public offering, according to multiple reports, after closing a financing round that values the company at approximately $965 billion — surpassing OpenAI's last known private valuation. The company that began as an AI safety spinout from OpenAI has grown into one of the most valuable private technology companies in history, driven largely by Claude Code's enterprise adoption. An IPO, if completed, would be among the largest technology listings in years.

Models

Google Gemini 3.5 Pro Enters Enterprise Preview With 2-Million-Token Context

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro is now available to enterprise customers in limited preview on Vertex AI, with general availability expected by June 30. The model features a 2-million-token context window — the largest of any generally available model — and full multimodal support across text and images. Gemini 3.5 Flash, which went GA at Google I/O in May, is already the default model in the Gemini app and AI Mode in Google Search, where it powers the company's rapidly expanding agentic search features.

Models

Microsoft Debuts MAI-Code-1-Flash — Its First In-House Coding Model

Microsoft unveiled MAI-Code-1-Flash, its inaugural proprietary model built specifically to generate source code from written descriptions. The announcement marks a significant strategic pivot: after years of deep reliance on OpenAI's models, Microsoft is now building its own AI stack for developer tools. The move directly targets the coding-assistant market where Anthropic's Claude Code has emerged as the dominant player, and where GitHub Copilot — previously powered exclusively by OpenAI — faces its most serious internal competition yet.

Business

SpaceX Acquires Cursor's Parent Anysphere for $60 Billion in All-Stock Deal

SpaceX announced a $60 billion all-stock acquisition of Anysphere, the company behind the Cursor AI code editor, in what is now the largest acquisition of a venture-backed startup in history. The deal, announced June 16, gives SpaceX a leading AI coding platform to potentially integrate across its engineering operations and the broader Elon Musk technology ecosystem. Anysphere had been valued at roughly $9 billion just months earlier, reflecting the extraordinary premium the market is now placing on AI-native developer tooling.

Policy

Amodei and Hassabis Call for U.S.-Led AI Coalition at G7 Summit

Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis jointly called for a U.S.-led AI coalition in a closed-door session at the G7 summit attended by around a dozen tech executives and multiple heads of state. OpenAI CEO Sam Altman was also present. The executives argued that democratic nations must coordinate AI governance before authoritarian-aligned AI systems reach strategic parity. Separately, the CEOs of OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and Microsoft AI signed a joint letter to Congress this month urging mandatory screening requirements on synthetic DNA providers — a rare display of cross-competitor alignment on biosecurity.

Infrastructure

Anthropic Claims 1 Gigawatt of Compute Capacity — Mostly on U.S. Soil

Anthropic announced it has secured 1 gigawatt of compute capacity through a combination of owned and leased infrastructure, with the majority sited in the United States. The figure is a striking marker of how quickly the leading AI labs have moved from pure cloud customers to infrastructure operators in their own right. For context, a single gigawatt is roughly equivalent to powering 750,000 average American homes. The buildout positions Anthropic to reduce its dependency on third-party cloud providers as it moves toward a public offering.

5 Patterns Worth Watching

  1. The AI talent war is entering a new phase. Jumper's departure from DeepMind is a bellwether. As Anthropic's valuation and prestige rise, expect more elite researchers to follow. The competitive advantage in AI is increasingly a function of who you can recruit — not just what you can compute.
  2. Coding AI is the battlefield that matters most right now. Microsoft building its own coding model. SpaceX paying $60B for Cursor. Google retooling Copilot. Every major player is betting that control of the developer workflow is the strategic chokepoint of the AI economy.
  3. Anthropic's IPO trajectory will reshape the industry. A near-trillion-dollar Anthropic going public sets a valuation benchmark that affects how investors price every other AI lab. Watch for OpenAI to respond with its own IPO timeline acceleration.
  4. The compute arms race is becoming infrastructure policy. Anthropic's 1 GW announcement is not just a technical flex — it's a geopolitical signal. The U.S. government's push to keep frontier AI compute domestic is filtering into every major lab's capital allocation decisions.
  5. Open-weight models are closing the frontier gap fast. MiniMax M3 combines a 1-million-token context window, native multi-modal computer use, and frontier-tier coding in an open-weight package. The cost and accessibility advantage of open models is converging with closed-model capability — a dynamic that will pressure every commercial lab's pricing strategy.
