Anthropic Raises Another $30 Billion — Its Second Mega-Round of 2026

Anthropic's annualized revenue doubled in twelve weeks. Now it has a near-trillion-dollar valuation and enough capital to fight the frontier war on its own terms.

Anthropic has closed a $30 billion Growth round led by Sequoia Capital, Dragoneer Investment Group, Altimeter Capital, and Greenoaks Capital Partners, pushing the company's implied valuation above $900 billion. Remarkably, this is Anthropic's second $30 billion raise of 2026 — the company closed an identical amount in February and has now taken in $60 billion in a single calendar year.

The fundraise reflects a business in genuine hypergrowth. Anthropic's annualized revenue run rate climbed from $14 billion in February to $30 billion in April — a near-doubling in just twelve weeks. That velocity, which outpaces even early OpenAI growth curves, is what justified a second record raise so quickly. Alongside the funding news, Anthropic confirmed a major compute deal with SpaceX and xAI, partnering on Colossus 1 infrastructure to secure GPU access independent of any single cloud provider.

The scale of capital flowing into Anthropic — and the speed at which it is being absorbed — signals that the frontier AI race has entered a phase where compute acquisition and talent retention require war-chest funding. Executives at Google, OpenAI, and Anthropic now openly describe the frontier race as "effectively neck-and-neck," with each lab making different tradeoffs around cost, speed, and raw compute.

Models

Google I/O 2026: Gemini 3.5 Flash Goes GA — Frontier Speed at a Fraction of the Cost

Google made Gemini 3.5 Flash generally available at Google I/O 2026, billing it as "frontier-level intelligence at 4× the speed of comparable models." Priced at $1.50 per million input tokens and $9 per million output tokens, with a 1-million-token context window, Flash is positioned as the model that runs inside products used by billions — not just researchers and power users. The launch reflects Google's stated strategy of staying at the frontier while keeping costs low enough for mass deployment across Search, Workspace, and Android.

Infrastructure

Gemini Spark: Google's Always-On Personal Agent Lives in the Cloud and Never Sleeps

The most ambitious product announcement from any lab this year came from Google: Gemini Spark, a personal AI agent that runs 24/7 on Google Cloud virtual machines and works autonomously across Gmail, Google Drive, third-party apps, and the open web — even when your device is off. Spark is Google's direct answer to OpenAI's Operator. Unlike browser-based agents, Spark operates as a persistent cloud process, enabling it to handle multi-hour tasks, monitor inboxes, draft responses, and manage schedules without requiring an active session.

Business

Google Bets $40 Billion on Anthropic — and Lands the Deal to Power Apple's Next Siri

Google Cloud CEO confirmed at I/O that Gemini will power a new, more personalized version of Siri launching later in 2026, with Google serving as Apple's preferred cloud AI provider for next-generation Apple Foundation Models. Separately, Google's cumulative investment in Anthropic now stands at $40 billion — the largest single strategic bet in AI history — underscoring a strategy of competing at the model frontier while simultaneously funding its most capable rival. The Apple partnership, if it holds, would give Gemini access to over 1.5 billion active devices.

Models

OpenAI Launches 'Frontier' Platform, a $4B Consulting Arm, and a Cybersecurity Program

OpenAI moved beyond model-provider in May with three simultaneous enterprise launches. Frontier is a new platform for building, deploying, and managing AI agents with shared context and integration into existing enterprise systems. DeployCo is a $4 billion consulting subsidiary designed to help companies implement AI at scale — a direct play for the professional services market. And Daybreak is a cybersecurity program combining GPT-5.5 with Codex to automate threat modeling, vulnerability identification, and patch generation. Three products, one message: OpenAI wants to be the operating system for enterprise AI.

Infrastructure

ChatGPT Connects to Your Bank — Plaid Integration Brings a Finance Dashboard to Pro Users

OpenAI rolled out a Plaid integration for ChatGPT Pro subscribers, giving the assistant read-only access to bank balances, transaction histories, investment accounts, subscriptions, upcoming bills, and savings goals. The result is a natural-language personal finance dashboard — users can ask "what did I spend on food last month?" or "am I on track for my savings target?" and receive answers grounded in live account data. The move extends ChatGPT's surface area from productivity tool to financial advisor, raising immediate questions about data handling and the long-term competitive threat to fintech incumbents.

Policy

Elon Musk Loses His OpenAI Lawsuit

A court ruled against Elon Musk in his lawsuit against OpenAI, which had alleged the company violated its founding nonprofit mission by transitioning to a for-profit structure. The ruling clears a significant legal overhang for OpenAI as it pursues its restructuring into a public benefit corporation and eyes a potential IPO later in 2026. Musk, who co-founded OpenAI before departing in 2018, had sought to halt the conversion and recover assets he argued belonged to the original nonprofit. The court found his claims insufficient to warrant relief.

America's Power Grid Is Being Rebuilt Around AI: NextEra's $67B Dominion Acquisition

NextEra Energy announced a $67 billion deal to acquire Dominion Energy, explicitly citing AI-driven power demand as the primary rationale — one of the largest utility mergers in history, justified directly by data center load growth. AI model training and inference are now consuming power at a scale that is reshaping the economics of the entire US electricity grid. The deal signals that infrastructure investors have moved from speculation about AI's energy requirements to large, irreversible bets on them.

  3. The agent era is no longer theoretical. Gemini Spark, OpenAI Frontier, and ChatGPT-Plaid all launched in the same month. The shift from AI as chat tool to AI as autonomous work system is the defining product transition of 2026.
  4. AI is restructuring physical infrastructure. A $67B utility merger justified by AI power demand is a new category of consequence. AI is now directly reshaping energy grids, not just software ecosystems.
  5. AI search is rewriting the internet economy. 58.5% of Google searches ending without a click is an existential challenge for every publisher, retailer, and advertiser whose business model depends on web traffic. The downstream effects are only beginning to surface.
