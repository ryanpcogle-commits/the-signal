Lead Story

OpenAI Files Confidential IPO Papers — and ChatGPT Just Hit 900 Million Weekly Users

The company that started as a nonprofit research lab has filed a confidential S-1 with the SEC, setting the stage for what could be the most consequential tech IPO of the decade.

OpenAI filed its confidential S-1 registration with the Securities and Exchange Commission earlier this month, targeting a public listing as soon as September 2026. The filing follows a March private-market valuation of $852 billion — a number that would make it the highest-valued company at IPO in history, surpassing even Saudi Aramco at the time of its listing.

The timing is deliberate. ChatGPT now serves more than 900 million weekly active users, with roughly one-fifth of all queries expressing direct commercial intent. OpenAI has announced advertising is now a "core part" of its business strategy — positioning sponsored experiences around usefulness rather than traditional attention-based metrics, a framing that distinguishes it sharply from Google and Meta.

Adding fuel to the IPO story: OpenAI previewed the GPT-5.6 series in limited partner access this week — three tiers named Sol (complex reasoning), Terra (daily-use scenarios), and Luna (low-cost large-scale inference). If the preview receives a strong reception, it could serve as a de facto product showcase heading into the IPO roadshow.

Anthropic Lands Nobel Prize–Winning Researcher from Google DeepMind

John Jumper — the Google DeepMind VP who won the 2024 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for his foundational work on AlphaFold — is leaving to join Anthropic. He is the highest-profile defection yet in an ongoing talent exodus from Google. Researchers Jonas Adler and Alexander Pritzel, both key contributors to Google's Gemini model, are also departing for Anthropic this week. The moves follow Nobel laureate Noam Shazeer's earlier jump from Google to OpenAI, signaling that the center of gravity in frontier AI research is actively shifting.

Models

Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 Represent a New Capability Tier

Launched June 9, Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 land at a capability tier Anthropic describes as "meaningfully above" Opus 4.8 — the previous performance ceiling. Fable 5 targets extended creative and multi-step reasoning tasks while Mythos 5 is positioned for the most demanding enterprise workloads. The dual-launch strategy mirrors the Sol/Terra/Luna tiering OpenAI is testing with GPT-5.6, suggesting the industry is settling on a three-to-four tier model architecture as the new standard.

Models

Google's Gemini 3.5 Pro Arrives with 2M Tokens and a "Deep Think" Mode

Google DeepMind shipped Gemini 3.5 Pro this month alongside the ultra-fast Gemini 3.5 Flash. The Pro model features a 2-million-token context window — the largest of any generally available model — and a specialized "Deep Think" cognitive mode that allocates additional compute to ambiguous or multi-step problems before producing an answer. Flash, by contrast, trades reasoning depth for speed and cost, targeting high-volume API workloads where latency and price per token matter more than deliberation.

Policy

White House Issues AI Innovation and Security Executive Order

The White House published a new executive order titled "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security" this week, establishing new federal guidelines on AI procurement, export controls on model weights, and a framework for assessing national-security risks from frontier models. Separately, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei and Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis joined OpenAI's Sam Altman at the G7 summit to call for a U.S.-led international AI coalition — a rare moment of unified advocacy from labs that otherwise compete fiercely.

Business

Microsoft Unveils Scout Agent and Two Proprietary AI Models

Microsoft launched Scout, an always-on autonomous agent built on the OpenClaw framework that operates across Microsoft 365 — Teams, Outlook, calendars, OneDrive, SharePoint — and executes multi-step tasks without a human in the loop. Alongside it, Microsoft released two proprietary models: MAI-Code-1-Flash for code generation and MAI-Thinking-1 for reasoning, both designed to undercut OpenAI and Anthropic on per-token cost. The move signals Microsoft is no longer content to simply resell partners' models — it's now a model maker competing in the same market.

Robotics

Boston Dynamics Puts Gemini Robotics Inside Spot — Spatial Reasoning Included

Boston Dynamics formalized a partnership with Google Cloud and DeepMind to integrate Gemini Robotics-ER 1.6 into the Spot robot dog and its Orbit AI visual inspection platform. The integration gives Spot enhanced spatial reasoning and continuous learning, enabling autonomous decision-making in complex industrial environments where conditions change between visits. The partnership is the clearest demonstration yet that foundation models aren't just powering chatbots — they are becoming the reasoning layer for physical machines operating in the real world.

Business

A24 Signs $75M AI Deal with Google DeepMind to Reshape Film Production

A24 — the studio behind Everything Everywhere All at Once and Midsommar — has entered a $75 million partnership with Google DeepMind to co-develop AI tools for film production and distribution. The deal makes A24 one of the first major studios to pursue a high-profile AI collaboration, signaling that Hollywood's initial resistance to generative AI is giving way to strategic investment. The tools are expected to assist with pre-production planning, post-production workflows, and audience analytics rather than replacing creative talent.

5 Patterns Worth Watching

  1. The talent war is no longer quiet. Nobel Prize winners and founding-team researchers are switching labs publicly. When the people building the technology start voting with their feet, it reshapes which frontier labs will lead in 12–24 months.
  2. Model tiering has become the product strategy. OpenAI's Sol/Terra/Luna and Anthropic's Fable/Mythos architecture signal a shift from single flagship models to tiered portfolios — optimized by cost, speed, and reasoning depth. Expect every major lab to adopt this playbook.
  3. OpenAI's IPO is the defining business event of 2026. An $852B valuation and 900M weekly users make this filing unprecedented. How public markets price "AI risk" will set the template for every lab that follows.
  4. Microsoft is now a model competitor, not just a distribution partner. MAI-Code-1-Flash and MAI-Thinking-1 put Microsoft in direct competition with OpenAI — the company it invested $13B in. The partnership is entering a new, complicated phase.
  5. Physical AI is finding its product-market fit. The Boston Dynamics–DeepMind integration and continued robotics investment from NVIDIA and others show foundation models are moving off-screen into industrial deployment. The next wave of AI ROI stories will come from factories and warehouses, not just offices.
