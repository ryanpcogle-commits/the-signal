Anthropic Is Closing a $30 Billion Round at a $900 Billion Valuation

With $44 billion in annualized revenue — up 80x in a single year — Anthropic has transformed from a safety-focused research lab into the most valuable private company in AI history.

Term sheets are not yet signed, but Anthropic is deep in negotiations for a $30 billion financing round that would peg its valuation at over $900 billion. The number is staggering, and the revenue backing it up is more so: Q1 2026 annualized revenue exceeded $44 billion, up from roughly $550 million just twelve months prior — an 80x increase year-over-year.

The enterprise traction is equally remarkable. Anthropic now counts more than 1,000 customers each spending $1 million or more annually. Among them: PwC, Blackstone, and Goldman Sachs — a who's-who of the Fortune 500 quietly building Claude into core workflows. The company's model lineup, anchored by Claude Mythos and the Claude 4.x family, has become the default enterprise AI stack for regulated industries that can't afford the reputational risk of less-safety-focused alternatives.

If the round closes at this valuation, Anthropic would be worth more than all but a handful of publicly traded companies on earth — and it hasn't held a single IPO roadshow.

This Week in AI
Models

Claude Mythos Became the First AI to Complete a 32-Step Cyber-Attack Range

Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview cleared a full 32-step end-to-end cyber-attack simulation — the first frontier model ever to do so. OpenAI's GPT-5.5 followed three weeks later. Rather than rush a public release, Anthropic limited Mythos to a curated group of security firms under a new initiative called Project Glasswing, which pairs the model with human red teams to identify software vulnerabilities before threat actors can exploit them. The controlled rollout is being watched as a template for responsible deployment of dual-use AI capabilities.

Infrastructure

Google I/O Eve: Gemini Is Now Inside Chrome for 3 Billion Users

With Google I/O kicking off tomorrow, May 19, the company has already pre-announced its most consumer-facing move yet: Gemini is now deeply integrated into Chrome, bringing contextual summarization, smart form-filling, and real-time translation to any webpage — no extension required. Google is also expected to unveil a new flagship Gemini model at the conference, benchmarked to land in the same capability class as OpenAI's GPT-5.5. The message to developers: Google's full-stack AI strategy — from its own chips to the world's most-used browser — is now vertically integrated.

Models

GPT-5.5 Instant Is Now the Default ChatGPT Model

OpenAI made GPT-5.5 Instant the default experience for all ChatGPT users on May 5th. The model scores 81.2 on AIME 2025 math benchmarks and 76 on MMMU-Pro multimodal reasoning — both category-leading numbers. The practical headline, however, is personalization: GPT-5.5 Instant can now search through a user's past conversations, uploaded files, and connected Gmail account to tailor its answers. For power users who have years of ChatGPT history, the model is starting to feel less like a search engine and more like institutional memory.

Policy

Pentagon Signed AI Deals With Eight Tech Giants — Then Reopened Talks With Anthropic

The Department of Defense finalized AI contracts with OpenAI, Google, Microsoft, and Nvidia — among others — while conspicuously excluding Anthropic. The move echoed the DOD's earlier labeling of Anthropic as a supply-chain risk. But the story has since shifted: after Anthropic announced several major technology breakthroughs, the White House reopened negotiations. The episode underscores the tension at the heart of enterprise AI policy — governments need the most capable models, and those capable models increasingly sit behind safety-first companies that won't accept every use case.

Business

Elon Musk Dissolved xAI's Independence and Handed Its Compute to Anthropic

On May 6th, Elon Musk announced that xAI would no longer operate as an independent company, and transferred a large share of xAI's AI compute capacity to Anthropic — one of xAI's most direct competitors. The move sent shockwaves through the industry. Analysts at Digitimes noted it signals that Anthropic's technical credibility has become impossible to ignore even for rivals. Grok 4.3, xAI's latest model, remains available, but without a dedicated compute base, its roadmap is now uncertain.

Hardware

Researchers Demonstrate 100x AI Energy Reduction Without Sacrificing Accuracy

A team published results showing that combining classical neural networks with human-style symbolic reasoning can slash AI energy consumption by up to 100x while simultaneously improving accuracy on complex tasks. The approach — sometimes called neuro-symbolic AI — has long been theorized but rarely demonstrated at this scale. If the results hold under replication, it redraws the economics of AI inference: data centers that today require gigawatts of power could potentially run the same workloads on a fraction of the grid capacity.

AI Overviews Now End 58.5% of Google Searches Before Users Click Anything

Google's AI Overviews now appear in roughly 20% of all searches, and the downstream effect is reshaping the entire web economy: 58.5% of Google searches in 2026 end without a single click to any website. For publishers, news organizations, and e-commerce sites that built their business models on organic search traffic, this is an existential shift. Microsoft AI chief Mustafa Suleyman added fuel to the debate this week, predicting that all white-collar work will be largely automated by AI within 18 months — a timeline that, if accurate, makes the search disruption look like a preview.

5 Patterns Worth Watching

  1. Anthropic's valuation trajectory is rewriting what "AI company" means. At $900B on private markets, Anthropic is worth more than most national stock exchanges. The 80x revenue growth in one year is not a startup anomaly — it's evidence that enterprise AI spend has passed the point of no return.
  2. Cybersecurity is the next AI frontier — and the most dangerous one. Mythos clearing a 32-step attack range is impressive. The same capability in the wrong hands is catastrophic. Project Glasswing's controlled rollout model may become the industry template for deploying dual-use AI responsibly.
  3. The web's click economy is collapsing. When 58.5% of searches resolve without a click, the implicit contract between Google and the open web — Google sends traffic, publishers create content — is broken. Expect regulatory scrutiny and new content licensing battles through the rest of 2026.
  4. The compute wars are producing strange alliances. Musk handing xAI's compute to Anthropic. Pentagon signing with OpenAI while reopening talks with Anthropic. Google building a full vertical stack. The landscape is no longer a simple competition — it's a web of interdependencies shaped by who controls the chips.
  5. Energy efficiency is becoming a first-order competitive moat. A 100x reduction in inference energy — if reproducible — changes which companies can profitably serve AI at scale. Whoever operationalizes neuro-symbolic efficiency first will have an enormous cost advantage over pure transformer-based competitors.
